If something is not working, or your question is not answered here, write to {{ site.contact_email }}. This is a one-person team; we usually reply within a few days. It helps if you include the app's version, which is at the bottom of the You tab.

How do I cancel my subscription?

The subscription belongs to your Apple Account, so it is cancelled through Apple. In the app, You → Subscription → Manage subscription opens Apple's subscription screen directly; choose Cancel Subscription there. The same screen is in Settings → (your name) → Subscriptions on your iPhone.

Cancelling does not cut the period you have already paid for; it simply does not renew at the end of it. Cancel a free trial before it ends and you are not charged.

How do I restore my purchases?

On a new phone, or after deleting and reinstalling the app, tap Restore purchases. It is on the paywall and in You → Subscription. You need to be signed in with the Apple Account you bought the subscription with.

How do I change my birth details?

You → Your details: tap the name, date, time or place row and change it. If you add a birth time later, your rising sign and houses are calculated. Changing a detail recalculates your chart and your content on the phone.

When do notifications arrive, and can I change the time?

There are two, each with its own switch in You → Notifications:

  • Morning quote — the day's quote, at the time you choose. 09:00 unless you change it, on the Time row of the same screen.
  • Weekly reading — Sunday evenings at 20:00, with the coming week's reading.

If you have not allowed notifications, the switch takes you to your iPhone's notification settings. Notifications are scheduled on your phone; nothing is sent from a server.

How do I add the widget to my Home Screen?

Touch and hold an empty part of the Home Screen, tap + at the top left, find {{ site.app_name }} in the list and choose a size. The small and medium widgets show the day's quote.

For the Lock Screen, touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customise, choose the lock screen, tap the area under the clock and pick {{ site.app_name }}.

Can I change how the widget looks?

Yes. You → Widget style: six themes (Sunset, Night, Cream, Gold, Ocean, Blush) and two fonts (Classic, Modern). Your choice applies to the small and medium widgets and to the quote image you share; you see a live preview on the screen and the Home Screen updates at once. The Lock Screen widget is drawn by the system and follows the font choice only.

How do I change the app's language?

You → Language: System language, Türkçe or English. The change applies at once, widget and notifications included. English content is written in its own pool, not translated.

How do I delete my data?

You → Your data → Delete my data, then one confirmation that lists what is removed: your birth details, your chart, the days and weeks computed for you, your favourite quotes, the widget's copy, your settings and any scheduled notification. The app returns to the state it was in when you first installed it.

The same link is at the bottom of the paywall if you have no subscription: deleting never requires paying. We hold no data about you and there is no account to delete (Privacy Policy).

Deleting your data does not cancel the subscription, which belongs to your Apple Account — see the cancellation steps above.
