Privacy Policy

{{ site.app_name }} app · Last updated: {{ site.last_updated_en }}

In short: everything you enter stays on your phone. There is no account, no server of ours and no analytics, and nothing is sold. Only two kinds of thing leave your phone: what goes to Apple for the place you search for and for your purchase, and — when we advertise the app — five events that measure which ad worked: the install, the app being opened, finishing the introduction, a trial starting and a subscription. Those events carry none of your birth details, your name or your quotes. We ask separately before using your advertising identifier, and the app works the same if you say no (see Ad measurement).

What the app keeps, and where

Only what you enter or choose in the app, and only on your phone:

  • Your name, used only to address you. You can leave it empty.
  • Your birth date, and your birth time if you know it. Without it, your rising sign and houses are not calculated.
  • Your birth place: the name, coordinates and time zone of the place you picked.
  • The two focus areas you chose.
  • Your favorite quotes: each quote's text and language, and the date you saved it.
  • Your settings: the notification switches and time, and the app's language.
  • Your content history: your birth chart, the daily quotes and weekly readings calculated for you, and which quotes you have already seen, so they are not repeated.

It is kept in the app's own storage and in the shared storage on the same phone that the home screen widget reads. Your birth details, your content and your favorites are left out of the phone's backups. Everything is calculated on the phone.

Nothing else. We do not ask for an email address, a phone number, your gender, your contacts, your photos or any health data. The app has no location permission and never asks where you are.

What we do not do

There is no account and no sign-in. We run no server that holds your data, and we keep no copy of anything. There are no ads inside the app. Your advertising identifier (IDFA) is used only if you explicitly allow it, and only for the ad measurement described below; say no and it is never read. We use no analytics and no crash-reporting service. We do not sell any data, and we share none of it with anyone for marketing. Apart from the ad measurement described below, we do not track you across apps or websites.

What leaves your phone

  • Place search. When you search for your birth place, the text you type is sent to Apple Maps to find it. When you pick a place, its coordinates are sent to Apple to look up its time zone. We attach no name or birth date to these requests. They are covered by Apple's privacy policy. With no network, the app offers to continue with the place name you typed and an approximate location; then nothing leaves the phone.
  • Purchases. Payment is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see, receive or store your payment details. Apple tells the app whether your subscription is active, and the app checks Apple's signed transaction on the phone with Apple's StoreKit.
  • If you contact us or open these pages. An email you send reaches us at {{ site.contact_email }} and is used only to answer you. These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages, which may record technical data such as your IP address under GitHub's privacy statement.

This version of the app downloads no content files, and the app never downloads or runs code: the program that does the calculations ships inside the app.

Ad measurement

When we advertise the app on Instagram, Facebook or TikTok, Meta's and TikTok's measurement libraries are included in the app so that we can tell which ad worked.

What is sent. Five events, each at most once: the install, the app being opened, finishing the introduction, a free trial starting, and a subscription being bought. The last two also carry three things, all from the App Store: which plan it was (the product identifier), its price, and the currency.

What is not sent. Your name, your birth date, your birth time, your birth place, your chart, your focus areas, the quotes you read or saved, and everything else. What an event may carry is a three-item list in the app's source code, and tests make it impossible to go beyond that list.

The permission question. After the free quote and before the subscription screen, once per install, a screen asks “Help us measure our ads?”; tapping Continue brings up the iPhone's own tracking permission question.

  • If you allow it: your advertising identifier (IDFA) may be used in the measurement, and which ad worked can be seen more exactly.
  • If you say no, or are never asked: the app works the same. No screen and no feature changes; the same five events are still sent, but your advertising identifier is not read. Measurement then uses Apple's SKAdNetwork and Meta's aggregated measurement, which work in aggregate rather than person by person.

You can take the permission back at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Both libraries' own automatic logging — including the parts that read purchases from the App Store receipt — is switched off, so nothing beyond the five events above is sent.

When we are not running a campaign. The libraries sit in the app but are never started: they run nothing, store nothing and connect to nothing.

Deleting. Delete my data also clears everything these libraries keep on your phone. Events already sent are on Meta's and TikTok's servers and cannot be recalled from the app; for those you would need to contact them directly. Meta's and TikTok's own privacy policies apply to that data.

Notifications

There are two, both optional, each with its own switch in the app's You tab: the morning quote, every day at the time you choose, and the weekly reading, every Sunday evening. The phone schedules them itself from content it already calculated. We use no push notification service; no device token is created or sent anywhere. Permission is requested only after you say yes in the app.

Deleting your data

Delete my data ("Verilerimi sil"), in the app's You tab and at the bottom of the subscription screen, erases everything listed above from the phone: your birth details, your chart, your calculated content and history, your favorites, the widget's copy, your settings and any scheduled notification. It happens immediately and cannot be undone; the app returns to the state it was in when first installed. Deleting the app removes it too.

There is nothing to request from us, because we hold nothing, and there is no account to delete. Your subscription belongs to your Apple Account: deleting your data does not cancel it (see the Terms of Use).

Your rights (KVKK and GDPR)

Because the person responsible is in Türkiye, Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK, Law No. 6698) applies. If you are in the European Union or the European Economic Area, you also have the rights the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) gives you.

These include the rights to know what data exists about you, to access, correct and delete it, to restrict or object to its processing, and to receive it in a portable form. Because your data exists only on your phone, you exercise them directly in the app: everything is visible and editable in the You tab, and deletion takes one tap, with or without a subscription. We hold no personal data about you on our side. The events described under Ad measurement go to Meta and TikTok; their own privacy policies, and their own processes for data-subject requests, apply to those. For any question, write to {{ site.contact_email }}.

You may also complain to a supervisory authority: in Türkiye, the Personal Data Protection Authority (Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurumu); in the EU or EEA, the data protection authority where you live.
